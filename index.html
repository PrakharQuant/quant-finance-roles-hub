Quant Developer — A Career Guide

Bridges quantitative research and production: building pricing libraries, low-latency systems, backtesting engines, and execution algorithms. Emphasizes C++ (95% interview weight), Python, systems design, concurrency, and production safety. Covers tech stack (KDB+, FIX, Docker), employers (Citadel, Jane Street, Goldman), specializations, career path from junior to head of trading tech, and compensation (highest in HFT).

Quant Portfolio Manager — A Career Guide

Transforms statistical signals into profitable portfolios and P&L. Responsibilities: signal ownership, budget allocation (VaR/ES), P&L accountability, team management. Terminology: alpha, IC/IR, Sharpe, capacity. Skills heavy on statistics, portfolio theory, programming, ML. Career from PhD researcher to PM in 8–12 years with multi-million compensation potential.

Quant Risk Manager — A Career Guide

Measures, stress-tests, and safeguards trading books. Models loss distributions (VaR, ES, Greeks, XVA), challenges valuations, defends capital under Basel III/FRTB. Skills: probability/stats (95%), stochastic calculus, Python/C++, regulatory fluency. Career from analyst to CRO; covers frameworks, employers, and modeling workstreams.

Investment Banking — A Career Guide

Intermediary role focused on M&A advisory, LBOs, restructuring, and capital markets underwriting (ECM/DCM). Organization into coverage and products. Skills: financial modeling, Excel, grit. Bank tiers, compensation, analyst-to-MD path, and exits to PE/HF/corporate development.

Actuarial Finance: A Quant Career Guide

Measures and funds uncertainty in insurance, pensions, and government. Covers life, P&C, health, pensions, catastrophe modeling. Skills: probability, financial math, R/Python, accounting/regulation. Credentials via SOA/CAS exams. Career from analyst to Chief Actuary with practice areas in pricing, reserving, ALM.

Currency Derivatives Trading

World’s largest market (~$9.6T/day FX). Instruments: forwards, NDFs, options, exotics. Skills: stochastic calculus, Python/C++, ML, pricing (Black-Scholes, SABR), risk (VaR, XVA). Alpha via carry, vol trading, HFT, macro. Career ladder from researcher to senior PM/desk head with P&L share.

Commodity Trading — Quant Career Guide

Trading real assets (oil, gas, metals, grains, power). Unique physical-financial hybrids, ~$13T+ notional. Skills: stats, stochastic calc, Python/C++, curves & logistics. Strategies: trend, carry, spreads, vol. Path from researcher/analyst to PM/trader; employers include Citadel, Goldman, Glencore.

Fixed Income Skills & Career Insights
